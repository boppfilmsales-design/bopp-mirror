     银色预涂膜（镀铝）

    银色预涂触感膜是软包装用膜，本薄膜经与纸张复合后，可在纸塑产品上进行彩色套印，UV上光，烫金等工艺；覆膜后有很好的银质感及层次感，还有独特的触感效果，

    主要用于礼盒，化妆品盒，酒盒，吊牌，电子器件等各种包装领域，

    如手机盒，香水盒，手提袋，酒盒等。

    Eva coating film (BOPP thermal laminating Film) glossy/matte were bopp bopet,bopa film coated with EVA on the surface, which were widely used in packaging lines such as: all kinds of wine boxes.

    Eva coating film included BOPET thermal film,BOPA thermal film, bopp thermal film

     

    Our goods features: high clarity, wearable on surface, durability good bonding.
