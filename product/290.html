  • Description

    Environmentally-friendly packaging film -POF Polyolefin Thermal (heat)Shrinkable Cling Film
    Types:
    1.) POF cling shink film -Center Folded (such as: 19mic x 45cm x 1000Mtr,30 mic x 585 mm x 1500 m)
    2.)  POF cling shink film - Tubular Form (such as: 25 mic x 330 mm x 1000 m)
    3.) POF macro perforated cling film
    Thickness :12-25 microns

    Five layers of co-extruded polyolefin stretch POF thermal(heat) shrinkable film, breaking the traditional three-layers co-extruded film and the traditional of hot-stretching process, featuring a higher toughness and higher anti-impact strength in the line of packaging film industry, and it is the most advanced environmentally-friendly plastic packaging material.
    Main features:
    High-resolution, glossy transparent, protection, and to prevent products subject to affecting of dust, moisture and odor without the appearance of erosion.
    Nice higher anti-crack-toughness and high anti-impact strength, keeping the relatively stable under high temperature and low temperatures, will not be broken, melted, broken ,decomposition and so on, suit for long-distance transportation and packaging of frozen products.
    faster and higher Shrinking rate, applicable to the packaging of various shapes and sizes of products, strong toughness, light weight, thickness uniformity, and economy.
    POF Thermal(heat)shrinkable film used for the beverage, food, medicine, packaging and other chemicals.
    Main products: single-layer membrane, folded membrane, small roll specifications,
